## The mental model

- **Warehouse:** accepted facts, findings, evidence and explicit uncertainty.
- **Library:** readable books/pages grounded in accepted knowledge. Facts can exist without a book.
- **Activity:** saved captures and work status; saved does not mean processed.
- **Review:** a human accepts or rejects proposed changes. An agent cannot approve itself.

Reading/search need no model account. A connected agent, supported transport and a live configured provider are not interchangeable readiness claims. See [connection limits](https://github.com/NewBww/knowledge-hub/wiki/Connections).

## Contribute safely

Edit versioned canonical docs first; see [Wiki Maintenance](https://github.com/NewBww/knowledge-hub/wiki/Wiki-Maintenance). For reproducible bugs, include synthetic steps and the exact source/app version. Never post live databases, captures, credentials or private screenshots. Report vulnerabilities through [Security](https://github.com/NewBww/knowledge-hub/wiki/Security), not a public issue.
